DESC:

The turning point for Baptiste was during a mission while Overwatch was still active, when he saw a strike team deployed to tend to the wounded soon after they left. It made Baptiste realize Talon's goal was not to end the conflict, but instead seeding chaos and profiting from it.

Baptiste pondered leaving many times, but he knew that wasn't a true option. As Mauga put it, they were in too deep, and when he tried to leave he would be chased until he was killed. Things would take a turn in Monte Cristi, where the team was deployed to eliminate the leader of a local Playa cartel, Daniel Fernandez. When they breached the crime lord's safehouse, he was nowhere to be found, so Cuerva ordered them to tear the entire town down until they found him. In the middle of the fire, Baptiste came across a small girl, scared to death as he was destroying her home, now a soldier no better than the Warbots that burnt down his own town back in the Crisis. In horror at what he had become. Baptiste lowered his rifle and fled the scene, finding a fishing boat at the dock. Before he left, he was confronted by Mauga, who had been ordered to find and retrieve him. As Mauga stared at Baptiste, knowing his friend would not come back peacefully, he communicated to Cuerva that he could not find Baptiste. Mauga allowed Baptiste to go, simply saying, "call me when you're ready to come home." While Baptiste was thankful for his understanding, he knew sooner than later, Talon would come after him again, and he needed to be ready.

It was entirely a localized problem.

Katya Volskaya happily complied, as her company fell into irrelevancy for years and saw this as the perfect chance to get back to the top. Zarya would quickly rise to become a Sergeant at the Novoanskoye Forward Base, and was personally given an experimental particle cannon, a weapon so heavy it could only be operated by Mechs, but for the strongest woman alive? It was lighter than a feather. Already a national icon from her Olympic career, Zarya's story moved the nation and gave many all over Russia the courage to enlist and defend their motherland together. In spite of this war effort, the Siberian Crisis would drag on for nearly a decade and result in the death of over 15000 lives, with no sign of stopping as the cause of the Omnium reactivating is still unknown.
