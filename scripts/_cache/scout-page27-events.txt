#261 Game On
year 2075 null |  | 
DESC:
The Exo Force Drones were able to hold back the Gwishin for a couple of years, but in due time, the Colossus adapted once more and began to disrupt the control networks. Without being able to control them remotely, the Korean Army was forced to come up with a new solution. The need to keep up with Gwishin adaptation led Dr. Lim to a similar solution to this familiar problem. Replacing the drones with reconfigurable mechs, inspired by Russia's resurgent Svyatogor technology. With these, a small task force of human pilots would be able to keep the war bots at bay with on-the fly improvisation and ever-changing equipment. Initial testing led to mixed results, with many dead and injured pilots. But when the team began to study the pilots that stood out from the rest, a common pattern became apparent, they were all avid video game players.

The controls of the Exo-Force mechs were not unlike those of most online shooters in the country, and their studies showed that professional players showed impressive reflexes and quick decision-making. The Korean Army decided to draft top talent from the Esports scene and similar fields with overlapping abilities. Mecha Guardian V champions Hana Song and Yuna Lee as well as their main rival, Kyung-soo Han. Seung-hwa Shi, a young analytical support specialist, and Jae-eun Kwon, a former F1 racer. 

Together, under the command of Captain Myung, they formed the current iteration of the MEKA squad, and finally granted Korea a consistent upper hand against the Gwishin. Their efficiency granted the team many liberties, and in concession to their professional backgrounds, they were allowed to customize the look of their uniforms and mechs to match their personal branding, using their professional name tags within battle and even allowing them to broadcast operations live from within the mech. While many of these broke military protocol, it allowed a massive boost in the program's popularity and opened the door to a massive propaganda machine, including several sponsors and movie deals for the different pilots.

#262 Neighborhood Hero
year 2070 2077 |  | 
DESC:
Even the top agents of Overwatch were shoved under the rug after the Petra's Act. Tracer was one of the cleanest, most beloved agents the organization had ever seen, and yet upon returning home she went from the face of the strike team, to a dull desk job at the RAF, a position she later learned was meant to be an intentional demotion.

Unsatisfied with the quiet life and lack of heroism in spite of the resume and abilities, she quit the military and began a new life with her new girlfriend, Emily, at an apartment in Wilmington Road. While she was happy with her new life and partner, complementing each other greatly and spending their nights playing long tabletop campaigns, she was only mostly happy. In reality, she still felt aimless without Overwatch, missing those days when she was making a difference. It didn't take long before she found herself on the move, becoming a local vigilante, righting wrongs wherever she still could, stopping thieves and helping the locals in their daily chores whenever the opportunity presented itself. Emily herself supported her activities, seeing them as compensatory heroics, but deep down she only wished for her love to be truly happy once more.

#264 Bounty Hunter
year 2070 2079 |  | 
DESC:
Overwatch agents all over the world found themselves blacklisted from almost every single field. It did not matter how many years of service they had done or their accomplishments outside the organization, for most of them, just being associated with Overwatch was enough reason to turn down an application. This reality hit Freja particularly hard, as she dedicated her life to Overwatch, measuring herself by the increasingly difficult missions she had grown used to. Without being able to find a job again and after facing constant rejection, her self-worth disappeared overnight.

That was until a local police department posted about a set of criminals on the loose, which finally made Freja realize that she could still be of use in this new era, just not under search and rescue. Freja began to take bounties in the criminal underworld of Denmark, starting with small jobs to track down and capture criminals behind the back of the law.

Over time, however, this became a full-time compulsion as she began to take bigger, riskier jobs yet again. While at first she limited herself to rescues and taking down criminals, as the money flew in and the rejected applications kept rolling, morality soon became irrelevant, and she continued to chase down bigger fish. She took on the pseudonym of Gefn to protect herself from her feelings towards dubious clientele, and as her notoriety grew, so did her fee. It may not be honest work, but that mattered little anymore. The world turned their backs on Freja, so she carved a new path as one of the most efficient bounty hunters in the whole world.

Still, this was not enough to satiate the Queen, who was now waiting to find the next target in her quest to rule the whole world. In the eyes of the Queen, the throne was where she said it was, and her kingdom started and ended wherever she felt like it, so while the Junkers are bound to the Outback right now, if the chance presented itself, they could begin to claim the rest of the world as their own.

DESC:
Cassidy had fallen far from Overwatch following Petra's accords. Over the years since Venice, he has grown to question his role in Overwatch, if they ever did enough good to outweigh the bad, or if Reyes merely turned him into a weapon for his own crusade instead. He figured Deadlock would want nothing to do with him anyway after signing up to be a part of Overwatch, and for as much as he felt out of place with the good guys, he would not even consider Talon as an option. So, instead, much like Freja, he became a gun for hire, fighting causes he believed were just to get by.

He made many enemies during his many jobs and often landed in local jail cells, eventually losing his left arm, though the story behind this is still unknown. During those outlaw days, he would also meet Sombra at a bar down in Mexico and use her as a way to gather Intel on jobs, like many other lost souls tended to do when they spend long enough in the shadows. He didn't stay in the same place for long, often hopping from one train to another to move across the States. While resting on a ride on a Houston-bound Hyperloop, he saw a Talon squadron attempting to take over the train. The Gunslinger noticed the troopers were using Blackwatch tactics, not going after him but instead searching for something inside the train.

If he did not act he would have this whole ordeal pinned on him, so he began to take down the troopers inside the wagons, but the soldiers kept coming without any signs of stopping. As he made his way down the train, he found an ominous glowing box, seemingly Talon's target. Without concerning much about the nature of the payload, he tossed it out of the wagon, confident that if Talon got to keep it they would leave the train and its civilians alone. Cassidy got to enjoy the rest of the ride as a proper lawful passenger, and when news broke out that the attack was being blamed on him, the staff kindly agreed to tell the truth to the police and fudge the arrival times enough to allow him to make an exit.

#268 Traitor

DESC:
After fleeing Talon, Baptiste sat in Tortuga for a while, but it didn't take long before Talon began sending agents after him. One after the other they would try to silence him, many of them Baptiste's old comrades. He wished to help make a better world in any way he could, to make up for his mistakes. For some people, this meant a band aid, for others, a bullet. He knew he couldn't erase the past, but he could at least make a difference where it mattered, so he began carrying out humanitarian efforts while keeping a low profile on his travels, trying to make a difference while still on the run.

DESC:
When Vishkar took control of Rio's renovation, the locks were told they were going to make Rio a better place, but it went down the same way all their other projects did. Failed promises, displacement and poverty, enforcing of curfews and ordinances to suppress local traditions, and even exploiting the populace for cheap labor. But there was still one silver lining for the people of Brasil in the form of Benicio Dos Santos.

Benicio was a local scientist who dedicated his life to the study of sound and its applications as a medium for healing technology. He had joined Vishkar so that, with their help, he would be able to fully develop his prototypes into something that could help his people. Instead, when he presented his inventions to Vishkar, they claimed ownership of the technology and adopted its more destructive capabilities into police equipment, to be used against protesters for quick and forceful neutralizing of anyone who got in their way. 

This technology was used even outside of Rio, later adopted by the London Police to suppress the Omnics of the Underworld, with Vishkar's own vice president Vaira Singhania adopting the technology into her assistive arms for personal protection. What was meant to be Benicio's gift to Rio was turned into yet another tool for oppression, and while Vishkar thought this was just like any of their previous projects, this time the water was beginning to boil, and the people of Rio were beginning to grow tired of their games.
