#281 Old Dragons
year 2077 null |  | 
DESC:
It had been around a decade since the Shimada brothers fought for the fate of the clan, a day Hanzo remembered like it was yesterday. He was now a lone wolf traveling aimlessly as self-punishment. He tried many ways to find peace with his actions, from meditational arrow carving to poetry, but deep down he knew that wouldn't redeem his mistakes. At first, the clan saw his abandonment as betrayal and sent assassins to kill him, so he used the chance to honor his skills with a bow, thinking that taking care of the Shimada would restore his honor, but once the clan died down, he found himself without purpose once more.  In spite of his roaming, every year he found time to visit Hanamura's castle on the anniversary of Genji's death, honoring him as penance. Every time he returned home, he saw more of what Hashimoto had made to the place, growing ashame of letting this happen, but too lost in his own grief to take care of the issue beyond dealing with thugs blocking his way into the castle. 

On the tenth anniversary of Genji's death, Hanzo broke into the Castle and took down the Hashimoto guards as usual, but as he lit his incense offering and prayed to honor Genji, he noticed a man wearing cybernetic armor had stalked him the whole time. The ninja taunted Hanzo, aware of his hypocrisy in honoring his brother in spite of being his killer, and it led to an armed confrontation in the main room of the castle as the two continued to argue over Hanzo's actions. The ninja argued that if Hanzo truly wanted to reclaim his honor, he should do it through his own actions rather than offerings. That revelation enraged Hanzo enough to trigger the Dragon Animas from his bow and shoot a lethal blow into the intruder, but to his surprise, his opponent carried an Anima weapon himself, using his dragon to bend Hanzo's strike back at him, forcing him to take the full brunt of the attack. 

Defeated and with the intruder's sword against his neck, Hanzo was ready to accept his death, but the ninja refused to grant his wish, because he still had purpose in life. It was here when the ninja referred to Hanzo as his brother, and while Hanzo did not believe him at first, when Genji unlocked his mask to reveal his scared face, it finally clicked with Hanzo that all this time his brother was alive. Genji did not stay long, simply claiming he was at peace with himself now and that he had forgiven Hanzo, but now it was time for Hanzo to forgive himself, as times were changing and Hanzo soon would need to pick a side in the world's struggles. As he prepared to leave, Hanzo called him a fool to think real life was like the stories their parents told, to which Genji said that even if he was a fool for believing there was hope for his brother, he nevertheless did, before vanishing on a smoke screen. With much to contemplate, Hanzo returned to finish his offering, with a new sense of purpose and focused as to where his path to redemption would take him next.

#282 The Last Bastion
year 2070 2077 |  | 
DESC:
In the middle of the black forest rested the remains of several Bastion units, defeated by the Crusaders and the German Army during the Crisis, slowly but surely reclaimed by nature, undisturbed for years. One day a miracle happened, a severely damaged unit suddenly reactivated, now overgrown with vines and roots, covered in rust and nested upon by many small animals including a particularly curious yellow cardinal.

The Bastion unit appeared to be conscious, like any Omnic awakened by the Crisis. Dr. Liao had long thought Bastions lacked the hardware requirements for sentience, which makes the nature of this Omnic's personhood still a mystery. The Awakened Bastion was filled with wonder about the beauty of the forest that surrounded him, and set out to discover its surroundings, growing attached to nature, and the cardinal that had been imprinted onto his chassis.

In spite of his gentle, even harmless demeanor, the Bastion unit was still dangerous. While Anubis' programming was nearly lost, the core combat capabilities of the machine were still functional and occasionally took over when Bastion sensed a threat. Not only that, but Bastion had scanned other units while scouting the forest, finding out his passage as a warbot and his final directive before being shut down to attack a nearby city. Luckily, Bastion's autonomy and his love for life and the wilderness distracted him from following this directive, choosing instead to remain with his animal companion, but as time passed, humans would begin to encounter the wondering Omnic, attacking him out of fear from the horrors of the Crisis. This led Bastion to largely avoid wandering in populated areas, remaining hidden in the European wilderness.

DESC:
Winston's recall was automatically sent not just to all Overwatch agents but also to their old bases, even those in disrepair. The signal reached Ecopoint: Antartica, although it was too weak to display in the damaged terminals. This message reactivated the remaining functional systems of the Ecopoint: and with it, Mei's cryogenic chamber opened. Nine years had passed since the base was lost to the super storm, yet Mei had not aged a day from the procedure. It took her a moment to realize she had been the only survivor in the whole base, now trapped in Antartica with nobody knowing she was alive.

Her only companion was the base's assistant drone, Snowball, and together they were able to get some of the systems running again, catching up to what had happened in the world and how Overwatch was disbanded. Mei knew that the power wouldn't last long, but if an emergency frequency by Overwatch had reached the Ecopoint, someone might be able to get them out, and with nine years worth of data of the uncontrolled anomaly prototype, it meant that if she got out, her team would not have died in vain.

With whatever parts she could find, Mei engineered a Cryogenic device that would help her climb up to the broken tower and catch the signal in her equipment, but the power gave how before it was completed. Seeing no other way out, Snowball hooked to the system and transferred its remaining battery to the base, buying Mei enough time to finish the device and climb up the tower. When the signal finally came into display, Mei was greeted by Winston's speech and able to answer the recall. After paying her respects to her deceased team, Mei left the Ecopoint: and ventured onto the continent, bringing Snowball along and hooking him to a solar panel. Together they traveled for days until they found a way out of Antartica and onto Gibraltar.
