DESC:
After years of struggling to accept his new body and his role with the Shimada downfall and Blackwatch, Genji was not wondering the world alone, at war with himself. Too often felt like he was not meant to be who he had become, and that his humanity died the day that Hanzo left him behind. Years alongside Overwatch agents, specially Angela, gave him a newly found appreciation for life and a desire to keep going, in spite of the ghosts of his past. Zenyatta would see Genji's struggle with accepting his new body and the man he had become, and decided to take him as a pupil, allowing him to deconstruct himself and achieve peace with himself once more. Genji would meditate with the Monks for years, with no one on the outside ever seeing Him again. He did, however, keep in contact with Angela, who he had grown to be extremely close. Even in the remote Himalayas, he found time to write her letters and keep in contact.

#243 Protecting the Skies
year 2070 2071 |  | 
DESC:
A certain private security firm had begun to make a name for itself around the Middle East, and quickly began to expand its profile internationally over the years. Helix Securities International, backed by the Oasis Ministries, quickly became the go-to company for many different organizations. Thanks to the research and development of experimental weaponry and equipment like the flying Raptora armor, they were able to land enough of an Impression to be hired, contracted by the United Nations, as the guard dogs of the many Overwatch facilities that were not in the process of being decommissioned. Their legitimacy was also boosted by the addition of Fareeha Amari into the Raptora Unit on the Giza Plateau, having been hired fresh out of leaving the army now under the Call sign of "Pharah". Whether the mission is to safely guard the remains of the Naughton Vault at Grand Mesa or deal with escaped experimental Bat-Wolves at Oasis, Helix was always up to the task, bringing justice from above.

#244 Code of Violence
year 2070 2072 |  | 
DESC:
The Zurich explosion had not actually killed off Jack or Gabe, they both vanished after the fact, becoming living ghosts. But Reyes was left in a far worse state, when Talon found his body. Dr. O'Deorain concocted a series of chemicals just to keep him alive, further destabilizing his body's molecular phasing. Reyes was living in a never-ending torment, unable to go back to the world of the living, but all things considered, also unwilling to try.

Having been failed by Overwatch, he decided to give Akande's offer a try, working for Talon alongside another operative, Sombra, to retrieve an asset at a New Mexico site. During the mission, Reyes realized Talon truly lacked any sense of structure, goal or loyalty. Everyone involved was just doing so for their own benefit, loosely fulfilling a shared goal. After clearing off the way into the base, they discovered Subject: Sigma, peacefully floating in maximum security, only to find out an extraction team led by the Widowmaker was to take on the mission from there, as they were still seen as rookies.

This lack of formalities made Reyes wonder what Takin truly was, a means to an end, held together purely by a code of violence. A group of individuals collaborating purely due to twisted desires leading them to work outside the law, and the perfect kind of environment for Reyes to act as judge, jury and executioner, after so many years of red tape getting in the way. Thus, he took on the mantle of the Reaper, wearing a mask to hide his past and becoming a harbinger of death, taking matters into his own hands and forming an expansive target list of individuals he aimed at removing, with the very last names being his Talon coworkers themselves.

#249 Top Player

DESC:
Years of competitive gaming shaped Hana's life when, just like her father, she decided to shape it into a career. Hana began playing Mecha Guardian V in high school, a massively popular online shooter with a strong E-sports presence in South Korea. Over the years, she quickly ascended through the rankings and became the number one player on the global scoreboard.

The two women had become celebrities across the gaming world, but Hana was usually in the spotlight, gathering a reputation for being a merciless opponent who played to win at any cost. D.va would become a household name among many Koreans and people from all over the world, both from her insane skills and actions per minute rate, as well as thanks to her blossoming streaming career, which kick-started her reputation. D.mon too, rose in popularity but was usually unaccustomed to the spotlight. While Hana showered in the glory of her wins, she would rather go back to assembling mecha figures in her dorms.
