#9 Greener Energy
year 2020 2040 | The Age of Progress | Bogota, Colombia
DESC:
As technology progressed, many fields of science quickly solved global issues such as illness and water scarcity, but it quickly became clear that the worsening climate of the planet would become the next major obstacle to surpass. Scientists all over the globe scrambled for ideas to produce cost-effective clean energy that could become a worldwide standard.

Dr. Justina Borda was one of the first to tackle this issue. A phycologist specialized in genetics, who sought to improve energy equity in her home of Colombia. Borda developed a strain of algae capable of thriving in low oxygen waters and photosynthesizing at disproportionate speeds. And after many attempts, she was able to get the energy produced by the algae to work alongside a Goldilocks Generator, taking the excess of electricity from the algae and essentially transforming any body of water into a solar panel.

Borda's invention spread across the world rapidly, as rural communities quickly standardized its use, to the point that even the most remote corners of the world have bodies of water where their creation managed to propagate. As her invention became the norm, the public moved away from fossil fuels for their daily energy consumption. Borda kickstarted a green energy revolution, and as superstorms became all the more common, the wind energy industry followed, which ultimately led to a decrease in carbon emissions of more than 20 percent during this period, giving humanity hope for a better tomorrow.

#10 Cleaner Oceans
year 2020 2040 | The Age of Progress | Honolulu, Hawaii
DESC:
While humanity was fighting to stop climate change, its effects were already heavily affecting people all over the world, and it was just as important to find solutions for those problems as it was for those to come. Island nations suffered the most from climate change, as the rising sea level and pollution of the oceans put their lifestyle at risk. For years, the people of the Polynesian region requested the international community to take the issue more seriously, but the handling of the situation left a lot to be desired.

This eventually led to the creation of the Polynesian League, an intergovernmental alliance between New Zealand, the Cook Islands, Samoa and the recently independent Hawaii, where together they would find solutions to the issues affecting their region.
