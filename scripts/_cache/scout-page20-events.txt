Their experimentation with a prototype machine capable of simulating abnormal weather seemignly influenced the formation of a huge blizzard trapped the team within the base in unlivable conditions, and made evacuation efforts extremely risky. These kinds of storms had happened before, and it had become standard procedure, but this particular blizzard was worse than any projections could have estimated.

As the storm raged on, the team remained in cryo-stasis, getting past the survival time window, where the system should have forced them to wake up, in the middle of the never-ending blizzard. Months passed, and the storm did not dissipate. Any attempts to establish communication failed, so the team was presumed dead. Overwatch would attempt to retrieve their bodies on multiple occasions, but the storm's anomalous persistence prevented access to the base for a full year. 


During one of the many rescue attempts, an Icebreaker ship, the Resolute, would crash into the Ice, and the rescue team barely managed to escape with their lives. The anomaly continued and Overwatch was forced to stop further retrieval attempts, lest they lose more agents. All of Overwatch entered a mourning period, with the Ecopoint bases rushing to improve security protocols in case of evacuation, and the UN calling for a full accounting of the records for review. Hector and Claudio joined their old partners in mourning the loss of the entire Antarctica team, and those who died trying to get their bodies back.
