Ecopoint: Connemara focused on the restoration of Irish boglands, later on collaborating with Russian scientists to aid in revitalizing the Siberian lowlands. Ecopoint: Osa in Costa Rica would focus on the recovery of reptiles and amphibians of the Corcovado cloud forest, seen as a bellwether of the rest of the natural world, focusing primarily on antifungals and biological solutions to waste treatment. And lastly, Ecopoint: Monterrey, a submarine base off the shore of California, meant to research marine life and follow protocols originally proposed by Leon Honisch to aid in the recovery of many species. The program was overall seen as one of Overwatch's most clean-cut successes and net positives to society at large, and a great example of the good they could do for the world

Gothenburg was able to avoid major damage, with Anubis focusing on Russia and the Ironclad defense systems. The city spent several months without major attacks. === #85 The Skycannon (2051) @ New York City, United States

=== #99 Bombardment (2051) @ Gothenburg, Sweden

=== #115 Australian Liberation (2053) @ Junkertown, Australia
After the crisis, most Omnium factories remained mostly intact, quarantined from Anubis' grasp, so most governors elected to keep the facilities around for study and potentially to find a better use for them.
